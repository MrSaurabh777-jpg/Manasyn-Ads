## 3. Non-negotiable compliance rules (from site disclaimer + prior briefs)

1. Manasyn is an **educational and self-help companion** — **not therapy, not diagnosis, not a replacement for a psychologist or psychiatrist, not an emergency service.** Say so in every product post.
2. **No cure claims, no outcome guarantees, no invented statistics, no fake testimonials, no invented clinician names.** Only numbers in §6 may be used.
3. Screening tools = educational, not diagnostic. Student tools = educational simulation, not accreditation/licence.
4. **iCall is NOT 24/7** — Mon–Sat, 10 AM–8 PM. Tele-MANAS 14416 is 24×7 (MoHFW). Emergency: 112.
5. Never imply affiliation with Tele-MANAS or any government service. Never optimise for "manas" alone.
6. No fear-mongering, shame, guilt, dark patterns, engagement-bait, fake vulnerability. No targeting minors (college+ framing).
7. **NMHS 2015–16 is a NIMHANS / MoHFW survey — NOT a WHO survey.**
8. Hashtag/wording caution: avoid `#AITherapy` and "AI therapy" as a claim (site says *"not therapy with a human professional"*). Use `#AICompanion`, `#MentalWellnessApp`, `#AICounselling` sparingly.
