## Notes on alt-text practice

- **Lead with the function**, not the decoration. A screen-reader user needs to know this is a stat card or a checklist before they need the motif described.
- **Never write "image of" or "graphic of"** — the screen reader already announces the element type.
- **Spell out numbers and symbols** in a way that reads naturally aloud (LinkedIn text fields handle digits fine; avoid raw "0–10" where "zero to ten" reads better).
- **Crisis footer numbers are in the caption, not the image**, so they are never trapped inside alt text — they stay selectable, copyable and updatable.
- **Devanagari script** (हिंदी / मैथिली / भोजपुरी on `day-03-postB`) is in the caption rather than relying on accurate rendering inside the image; the on-image text for that card is English.
