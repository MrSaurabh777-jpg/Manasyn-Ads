Class 12 ya BA first year mein jab pehli baar Freud padha tha, toh ek hi cheez samajh aayi thi — "Id, Ego, Superego" — aur exam mein teeno ko mix kar diya tha. 🙈

Freud ke hisaab se hamara mann ek iceberg jaisa hai. Upar ka chhota hissa — jo abhi soch rahe hain (conscious). Paani ki line ke paas — jo yaad kar sakte hain (preconscious). Aur neeche ka bada hissa — woh ichchhaayein, darr aur yaadein jo humne bahar dhakel di, lekin jo aaj bhi hamare faislon ko chala rahi hain (unconscious). 🧊

Phir personality ke teen hisse:
Id — bachpan se hai. "Abhi chahiye." (Pleasure principle)
Superego — parents aur samaaj se seekha. "Yeh galat hai, sharam karo." (Morality principle)
Ego — beech ka referee. "Chalo, koi practical raasta nikaalte hain." (Reality principle)

Exam ki raat wala scene sochiye: Id kehta hai "ek episode aur", Superego kehta hai "Papa ka bharosa tootega", aur Ego deal karata hai — "20 minute break, phir 2 ghante padhai." Jab Ego deal nahi kara paata, tab anxiety aati hai.

Aur anxiety se bachne ke liye Ego 8 tarah ki dhaalein (defense mechanisms) use karta hai — jaise boss ka gussa chhote bhai pe nikaalna (displacement), ya "woh company waise bhi achhi nahi thi" (rationalisation). Sab 8 examples ke saath slides 5–6 mein hain, aur slide 7 pe yaad rakhne ki trick.

Ek zaroori baat: Freud ki kai baatein aaj science mein test nahi hoti, lekin defense mechanisms par research aaj bhi ho rahi hai (Vaillant, 2000; Cramer, 2015). Isliye theory bhi padhiye, criticism bhi.
