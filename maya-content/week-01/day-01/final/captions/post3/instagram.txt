Id, Ego, Superego — teen naam toh yaad ho jaate hain. Matlab nahi. 🧠

Mujhe bhi confuse karta tha… jab tak maine yeh notes nahi banaye. Swipe karo →

Freud ne mind ko teen layers mein baanta: jo abhi dikh raha hai (conscious), jo yaad kar sakte ho (preconscious), aur woh bada hissa jo paani ke neeche hai — wishes, fears, memories jo bahar dhakel di gayin, lekin gaadi wahi chala rahi hain (unconscious). 🧊

Phir teen players:
• Id — "abhi chahiye." (pleasure principle)
• Superego — "sharam karo." (morality principle)
• Ego — "chalo deal karte hain." (reality principle)

Exam ki raat yeh teeno aapke andar ladte hain — slide 4 mein poora scene hai. 😄

Jab Ego deal nahi kara paata, anxiety aati hai. Aur tab Ego apni 8 dhaalein nikalta hai — defense mechanisms. Repression se Sublimation tak, har ek ka ek desi example slides 5–6 mein.

Aur slide 7 pe woh trick jo exam hall mein kaam aayegi:
I·E·S = Instinct, Executive, Standards.
Defenses = DOWN · OUT · DRESS · BACK/UP. 📝
