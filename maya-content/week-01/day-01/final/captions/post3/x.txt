1/ Id, Ego, Superego: everyone memorises the names, nobody remembers what they do.

Freud's whole model in 6 tweets, with the mnemonic that finally made it stick 🧠

2/ The mind is an iceberg.
Tip = conscious (what you notice now)
Waterline = preconscious (what you can recall)
Underwater = unconscious (wishes, fears, memories pushed out of view — still steering you)

Fun fact: Freud never used the iceberg image himself (Green, 2019).

3/ Three players:
Id → "I want it NOW" (pleasure principle, born with it)
Superego → "You SHOULD" (morality; parents + society)
Ego → the referee (reality principle) — satisfies the Id without angering the Superego

4/ Exam night:
Id: "one more episode"
Superego: "you'll disappoint everyone"
Ego: "20-min break, then 2 hours, sleep by 11:30"

A strong Ego negotiates. When it can't, the clash = anxiety.

5/ Anxiety triggers the Ego's shields — defense mechanisms (Anna Freud, 1936):
🔒 Repression — memory pushed out of awareness
🙈 Denial — "I'm not stressed" (nails bitten raw)
🪞 Projection — "I'm not jealous, THEY are"
↪ Displacement — boss yells → you snap at your sibling

6/ Four more:
🎀 Rationalisation — "that company was bad anyway"
🎭 Reaction formation — like someone → act rude to them
🧸 Regression — stress → childlike sulking
🌱 Sublimation — anger → gym, cricket, poetry (the healthy one; Vaillant, 2000)

7/ The mnemonic:
I·E·S = Instinct (Id), Executive (Ego), Standards (Superego)
Defenses = DOWN (repress, deny) · OUT (project, displace) · DRESS (rationalise, react) · BACK/UP (regress, sublimate)
