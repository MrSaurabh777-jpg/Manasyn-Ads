Title: 5 Science-Backed Sleep Habits for Better Mental Health (Why One Bad Night Makes You 60% More Reactive)

Description:
Struggling with mood swings, anxiety or irritability? Check your sleep first. Research shows one night of poor sleep makes the brain's emotion centre over 60% more reactive (Yoo et al., 2007), and improving sleep improves depression, anxiety and stress (Scott et al., 2021). Save these 5 sleep hygiene tips for better mental health tonight: 1) Get 10 minutes of morning sunlight. 2) Fix your wake-up time — even on weekends. 3) Stop caffeine (chai, coffee, cola) by 3–4 PM. 4) Use the 20-minute rule: can't sleep, get up until drowsy. 5) Write tomorrow's to-do list before bed. Simple sleep routine ideas for students, professionals and night owls in India.
