Aaj subah uthte hi mood kharab tha? Chhoti si baat pe gussa aa gaya? Pehle apne aap se ek sawaal poochiye — kal raat kitne ghante soye the? 🌙

Hum neend ko "aaram" samajhte hain. Science kuch aur kehti hai: neend woh time hai jab dimaag din bhar ki emotions ko process karta hai aur unka "volume" kam karta hai.

Ek famous brain-scan study mein logon ko ek raat jagaya gaya. Agle din unka emotion centre (amygdala) negative tasveeron pe 60% se zyada react kar raha tha, aur use shaant rakhne wala prefrontal cortex theek se connect nahi ho raha tha (Yoo et al., 2007). Isiliye kam neend ke baad sab kuch "zyada" lagta hai — zyada bura, zyada personal.

Achhi khabar: yeh raasta dono taraf chalta hai. Jab neend sudharti hai, mood, anxiety aur stress bhi sudharte hain — 65 studies ke ek bade review ne yahi paaya (Scott et al., 2021).

Ek aam galatfehmi: "Main 5 ghante ka aadi hoon." Research mein log 2 hafte roz 6 ghante soye — unhe neend "theek" lagne lagi, lekin concentration roz girti rahi (Van Dongen et al., 2003). Hum adapt nahi karte, bas pata chalna band ho jaata hai. Adults ke liye 7+ ghante recommended hain (American Academy of Sleep Medicine, 2015).

5 chhote anchors jo aaj raat se shuru kar sakte hain:
1. Subah 10 minute ki dhoop — balcony ya chhat kaafi hai.
2. Uthne ka time fix rakhiye — Sunday ko bhi. Body clock sone se nahi, uthne se set hoti hai.
3. Shaam 4 baje ke baad chai-coffee nahi. Caffeine 6 ghante baad bhi neend ko todti hai (Drake et al., 2013).
4. Bistar pe 20 minute mein neend nahi aayi? Uthiye, halki roshni mein kuch boring kijiye, wapas tab aaiye jab aankhein bhaari hon.
5. Sone se pehle kal ke kaamon ki list likh dijiye — dimaag ko "yaad rakhne" ki duty se chhutti mil jaati hai (Scullin et al., 2018). 📝
