Kal raat 4–5 ghante soye, aur aaj har chhoti baat pe chidh ho rahi hai? 😤

Yeh aapka "character" nahi hai. Yeh aapka amygdala hai.

Ek brain-imaging study mein, sirf EK raat ki neend chhodne ke baad dimaag ka emotion centre (amygdala) negative cheezon pe 60% se zyada react karne laga — aur usse control karne wala prefrontal cortex "disconnect" ho gaya (Yoo et al., 2007). 🧠

Matlab: kam neend = brakes off, accelerator on.

Aur ulta bhi sach hai: 65 trials ke meta-analysis mein neend sudhaarne se depression, anxiety, rumination aur stress — sab mein sudhaar aaya (Scott et al., 2021). Neend bonus nahi, foundation hai. 🌙

Myth: "Main 5 ghante ka aadi ho gaya hoon."
Sach: 2 hafte 6 ghante sone waalon ko LAGA ki woh theek hain — performance roz girti gayi (Van Dongen et al., 2003). Adapt nahi hote, bas pata chalna band ho jaata hai.

5 sleep anchors jo aaj raat se kaam karte hain: ☀️
1. Subah 10 min roshni — balcony, chhat, walk.
2. UTHNE ka time fix — Sunday bhi. Sone ka nahi, uthne ka.
3. Chai/coffee cutoff 3–4 PM (6 ghante pehle wali caffeine bhi neend todti hai — Drake et al., 2013).
4. 20-minute rule: neend nahi aayi toh utho, dim light, boring kaam, wapas tab jab aankhein bhaari hon.
5. Brain-dump: kal ki to-do list likh do — log 9 min jaldi soye (Scullin et al., 2018). 📝
