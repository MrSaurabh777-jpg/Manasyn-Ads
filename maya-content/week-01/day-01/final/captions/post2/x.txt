1/ One bad night of sleep and suddenly everything feels personal?

That's not your personality. It's your amygdala.

A thread on why sleep is the cheapest mental-health tool you own 🧠

2/ In a brain-imaging study, ONE night without sleep made the amygdala (the emotion centre) over 60% more reactive to negative images — while the prefrontal cortex, which normally calms it, disconnected (Yoo et al., 2007).

Brakes off. Accelerator on.

3/ It works the other way too.

A meta-analysis of 65 randomised trials found that improving sleep improves depression, anxiety, rumination AND stress (Scott et al., 2021).

Sleep isn't a self-care bonus. It's the foundation.

4/ Myth: "I've adapted to 5–6 hours."

Reality: after 2 weeks at 6 hrs/night, people FELT fine while their performance kept dropping every day (Van Dongen et al., 2003).

You don't adapt. You stop noticing.

5/ Five anchors that work tonight:
☀️ 10 min morning light
⏰ Fixed WAKE time, even Sunday
🍵 Chai/coffee cutoff 3–4 PM (Drake et al., 2013)
🛏 Not asleep in 20 min → get up, dim light, return when sleepy
📝 Write tomorrow's to-do list before bed (Scullin et al., 2018)

6/ Pick one. Not five — one.
