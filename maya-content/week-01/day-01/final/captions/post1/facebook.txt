Ek scene describe karte hain — dekhiye kitna familiar lagta hai.

Raat ke 1 baje. Ghar mein sab so chuke hain. Aap bistar pe hain, phone haath mein, aur dimaag band hone ka naam nahi le raha. Kal ka kaam, kisi ki baat jo chubh gayi, paise ki tension, ya bas ek bhaaripan jiska koi kaaran samajh nahi aata.

Aap sochte ho — kisi se baat kar loon? Lekin phir: "Itni raat ko kaun uthega? Aur bolunga kya — 'mujhe pata nahi kya ho raha hai'?"

Isi "pata nahi kya ho raha hai" ke liye humne Manasyn ka AI Companion banaya hai.

Yeh ek chatbot nahi hai. Yeh ek guided check-in hai — jaise ek samajhdaar dost jo sahi sawaal poochta hai: "Neend kaisi chal rahi hai?" "Din mein kab sabse zyada bhaari lagta hai?" — jab tak baat saaf na ho jaaye.
