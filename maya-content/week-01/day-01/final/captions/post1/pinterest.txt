Title: Can't Switch Off Your Mind at 1 AM? Try a Guided Mental Health Check-In (Hindi, English, Maithili, Bhojpuri)

Description:
Late-night overthinking, racing thoughts and "who do I even talk to?" — sound familiar? Manasyn's AI Companion is a guided mental health check-in, not a chatbot. It asks the right next questions about your mood, sleep and stress, in your own language (English, Hindi, Maithili, Bhojpuri or Hinglish), and creates a simple report you can share with your psychologist. How it works: 1) Open manasyn.app on any phone browser — no download. 2) Pick your language and start a calm, structured check-in. 3) Get a short summary you can save or share with a professional. Free during private beta. Self-help and educational only — not therapy or diagnosis; if you're in crisis in India call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416.
